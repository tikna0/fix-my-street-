FIXMYSTREET

1\. What is FixMyStreet?

FixMyStreet is a website designed to help people report public problems in their local area. Examples include potholes, damaged roads, garbage, broken streetlights, and water leaks.

The system uses AI to help identify civic issues and predict their priority. This helps organize reports so the responsible team knows which problems need attention.

2\. How Does It Work?

Step 1: Report a Problem

A resident opens the website and reports a problem. They provide details such as the issue description, location, and a photo if the form supports it.

Step 2: AI Identifies the Issue

The AI feature helps identify the type of problem. For example, it helps classify a report as a pothole, garbage issue, or damaged streetlight.

Step 3: Predict the Priority

The system estimates how urgent the issue is. This helps the team identify reports that need faster attention.

Step 4: Review the Report

An administrator or responsible civic team reviews the report and decides what action to take.

Step 5: Resolve the Issue

The team works on the problem and updates its status if the platform supports progress tracking.

3\. Who Uses FixMyStreet?

Residents: Residents report problems in their neighbourhood and follow their progress if tracking is available.

Administrators: Administrators review reports, organize issues, and manage submissions.

Civic Teams: Responsible teams review reported problems, decide on actions, and coordinate repairs.

4\. Main Benefits

1\. Easy reporting. People have a central place to report public problems.

2\. AI assistance. AI helps classify issues and estimate their priority.

3\. Better organization. Reports stay organized for easier review.

4\. Faster decision-making. Priority estimates help teams decide which reports need attention first.

5\. Better communication. Status updates, if available, help residents follow progress.

5\. Example of How It Works

Imagine a resident finds a pothole on a road.

First, the resident submits a report with the location and a photo.

Next, the system helps identify the issue as a pothole and estimates its priority.

An administrator reviews the report and forwards it to the responsible team.

The team inspects the road and arranges the repair.

The issue status is updated as work progresses, if status tracking is available.

6\. Simple Explanation to Tell Your Client

“FixMyStreet is a civic issue reporting platform designed to help people report public problems such as potholes, garbage, damaged roads, and broken streetlights.

The platform uses AI to help identify reported issues and predict their priority. Administrators review the reports and coordinate with the responsible teams to address the problems.

The main goal is to make reporting easier, organize civic complaints, and help local authorities manage public issues more efficiently.”

7\. Overall Goal

FixMyStreet aims to connect residents with the teams responsible for maintaining public spaces. It combines issue reporting and AI-assisted prioritization to support better management of local problems.
